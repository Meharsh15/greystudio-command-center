# GreyStudio Command Center AI Context

GreyStudio is a small creative studio and walk-in shop moving toward a creative marketing agency.

Core revenue lines: website development, graphic design and branding, photography, video editing, printing, Xerox, passport photos, frames and money-transfer commission.

Financial operating baseline:
- Survival baseline: ₹28,300/month
- Recorded debt target: ₹6,75,000
- Earlier repayment horizon: 12 months
- Earlier monthly debt pace: ₹56,250
- Long-term profit target: ₹2–5 lakh/month

## Daily closing
At the end of each day, record three actual account snapshots: Cash Counter, Bank Account and AEPS Account.
Daily closing is a balance snapshot, not revenue. It is stored by date so historical closing balances remain visible.
Never invent a balance. Never replace an actual closing amount with a calculated estimate unless the user explicitly asks for an estimate.

## Quick ledger grammar
Incoming: 100+, +100, 100v, V100
Outgoing: 100-, -100, 100p, P100
Optional reason/category follows the amount.

## AI safety
Database facts win over memory. Never treat a lead, quote or forecast as collected cash. Never mutate transactions, balances, debts or tasks merely because the AI suggested it. Record-changing actions require explicit approval. Never reveal secrets or database credentials.
