# GreyStudio Command Center — AI Context

GreyStudio is a professional studio and walk-in shop being developed into a creative marketing agency.

## Business model
Creative services: website development, graphic design, photography, video editing and print jobs.
Walk-in defense services: money transfer commission, Xerox, passport photos, photo frames, printing/stationery.
Digital asset: gold-rate information site with approximately 4,000–6,000 monthly visitors; monetization is planned but should not be counted as current cash until revenue is actually recorded.

## Financial baseline
### Monthly essential cash requirement
Shop rent ₹6,800 + shop supplies ₹3,000 + groceries ₹5,000 + fuel ~₹3,500 + EMIs ₹2,900 + ₹4,100 + ₹3,000 = ₹28,300/month.

### Employment/income reality
Job salary ₹36,000/month.
Co-operative deduction ₹26,166/month.
Bank credit after deduction ₹10,474.
The supplied plan treats usable daily job income as ₹0 because the remaining salary is absorbed by the wider debt/NPA situation.
Reference salary-based gap: ₹17,826.
Current operating plan therefore asks GreyStudio to fund the full ₹28,300 survival baseline.

### Debt
₹6,00,000 + ₹65,000 + ₹10,000 = ₹6,75,000 zero-interest liabilities.
12-month planning horizon.
Approximate principal pace: ₹56,250/month.

### Targets
Walk-in counter net reference: ₹18,000–₹22,000/month.
Studio cash generation for survival + debt: ₹75,000–₹85,000/month.
Arithmetic reference from survival plus debt pace: ₹84,550/month.
Daily baseline defense reference: approximately ₹950–₹1,000/day net.
Long-term target: ₹2–₹5 lakh/month net profit.

## Strategy
Defense: make counter services reliably cover operating essentials.
Growth: pursue high-ticket B2B website, branding and commercial creative/video work.
Monetization: convert gold-rate website traffic into advertising, affiliate and local jeweler sponsorship revenue.
Capital rule: do not recommend new debt for speculative expansion.
