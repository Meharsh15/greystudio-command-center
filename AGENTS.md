Core creative services: website development, graphic design, photography, video editing and print jobs.

## Quick-entry grammar
Incoming: `100+`, `+100`, `100v`, `V100`.
Outgoing: `100-`, `-100`, `100p`, `P100`.
Optional reason follows the amount, e.g. `500+ passport`, `20p tea`.
The mobile calculator is the preferred fast-entry UI: enter amount, choose RECEIVE (+) or PAID (-), then enter a reason or use a shortcut such as MT, PS or X/P and press Enter/Save.
